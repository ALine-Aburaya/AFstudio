AF Studio – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 4, 2026

AF Studio is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It creates short product videos and, when the user connects their own TikTok account, uploads those videos to that account as drafts or posts at times the user schedules.

Use

  • The app is used only by its owner to manage their own TikTok account.
  • Every upload is started by the user; the app never posts without the user scheduling it.
  • The user is responsible for the content they publish and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

TikTok account

The user can disconnect the app at any time in the app itself or in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
